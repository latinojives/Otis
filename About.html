GMI Global Partners was founded on a single conviction: that the world's most consequential credit opportunities exist at the edges of where traditional capital is willing to go. We exist to close that gap — with discipline, integrity, and institutional rigour.

Our Story

We began with a question that most institutions were not asking: why does disciplined, institutional-grade capital rarely reach the places that need it most?

The answer, we found, was not risk. It was proximity.

GMI was established in Houston, Texas — a city forged by the intersection of energy, enterprise, and global capital flows. From the start, we operated differently. Where others saw complexity as a barrier, we built the expertise to navigate it. Where others saw emerging markets as peripheral, we saw them as the frontier of the next generation of credit opportunity.

Today, GMI is one of the few private credit managers with genuine operational presence across these geographies — not as a tourist, but as a long-term capital partner.

Milestones

A Decade of Deliberate Growth

2013
Founded in Houston, Texas
GMI Global Partners is established with a focus on private credit and structured finance for North American mid-market corporate borrowers and financial sponsors.
2016
New York & London Offices Open
Expanding access to global markets, the firm establishes institutional presence on both coasts of the Atlantic — deepening relationships with European sponsors and cross-border M&A deal flow.
2019
First Sovereign Credit Allocation — MENA
GMI completes its first significant allocation to Gulf sovereign and quasi-sovereign debt instruments, establishing the firm's emerging market credit capabilities and research infrastructure.
2021
Fintech Credit Platform Launched
GMI launches its structured credit facility offering for digital lenders and fintech platforms — becoming one of the first institutional private credit managers to formally address the capital needs of next-generation financial institutions at scale.
2023
Africa Strategy Commences
The firm partners with an African financial institution and formally initiates its Pan-African private credit strategy, beginning due diligence across Sub-Saharan sovereign, banking, and corporate credit markets.
2026
GMI Fund Africa — Active Pipeline
GMI Fund Africa enters active deployment phase across six Sub-Saharan markets. The firm surpasses $4B in cumulative capital deployed globally and deepens its Hong Kong presence to serve Asia-Pacific institutional allocators.
