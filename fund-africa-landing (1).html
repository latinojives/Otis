The Investment Case

GMI Fund Africa

The anchor vehicle of the GMI Africa platform — an evergreen, multi-series structure engineered for scale, disciplined underwriting, and institutional-grade governance.

Investment Mandate

Fund Africa channels institutional capital directly into Africa's top-tier regulated financial institutions, sovereigns, and central banks through a diversified, credit-first mandate spanning corporate lending, infrastructure, energy, financial services, and TMT.

Strategic Positioning

As the anchor vehicle of the GMI Africa platform, Fund Africa is built for scale, disciplined underwriting, and institutional-grade governance. GMI holds a singular mandate to execute this program at this scale across the continent — a position management believes is unmatched among platforms operating at comparable size and structure.

Institutional Highlights

  • 80% private credit / 20% public & hedge-fund absolute return sleeve
  • First-lien structural seniority across all deployments
  • Tier-1 credit insurance wrap via Insurance Services
  • U.S. regulated evergreen fund structure
  • U.K. multi-series managed vehicle
  • Cayman Islands SPC with segregated portfolio protections
  • Minimum LP commitment of USD $50 million
